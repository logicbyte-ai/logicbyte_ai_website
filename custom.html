The build-versus-buy gap

Off-the-shelf tools stop
where your problem starts.

Every field has images holding answers no generic tool was built to extract — a modality it never anticipated, a sample it can’t handle, a decision it can’t defend. The usual fallbacks are a black box you can’t audit or a script that dies the day its author leaves. Neither is a capability.

No tool fits your imaging

A novel instrument, an unusual sample, a new contrast mechanism or acquisition protocol — off-the-shelf software was never designed for it, and forcing your work through a generic pipeline quietly costs you accuracy you can’t see.

A black box you can’t defend

Regulated submissions, quality-critical inspection and reproducible science need every step documented and traceable. A result you can’t reproduce — or explain — is a result you can’t stand behind.

Expertise you have; engineering you don’t

The imaging knowledge is yours. Turning it into a production-grade, maintainable pipeline is a different discipline — and a pipeline that lives in one person’s head isn’t a capability, it’s a risk.

The domain-agnostic toolkit

Every step, from raw frame
to the decision.

Compose the exact pipeline your problem needs from a deep library of operations that travel across fields — and when the operation you need isn’t there, we build it. Preview any of them on your own images, then license only what your work demands.

Restore & enhance

Recover signal from difficult images.

Denoising that is classical, AI-guided and hybrid — NLM, bilateral, wavelet, total-variation, BM3D and anisotropic diffusion. Illumination and contrast correction, CLAHE, destriping and ring-artefact removal, and frequency-domain (FFT notch and bandpass) filtering for clean, comparable inputs.

Expanded

Detect, segment & measure

Find every object; measure it in real units.

Otsu, multi-Otsu and adaptive thresholding; morphology and connected-component labelling with per-object statistics; ridge and line filters for cracks, fibres, vessels and filaments. Calibrated area, distance and interactive line-intensity profiles — read straight from your metadata.

New

Classify & grade

Decisions trained on your data.

Supervised deep learning for object-level classification, grading or categorisation — trained on your own annotated images, so the model learns your problem rather than approximating it.

Anomaly & change detection

Flag the new, the odd, the changed.

Unsupervised anomaly detection that needs no defect library — ideal for novel failure modes and new modalities. Plus phase-correlation alignment and two-image difference for precise before/after change detection.

Quantify, plot & export

From one object to a whole population.

Per-object and population statistics, enumeration and texture analysis with batch processing built in — then plotting (histogram, scatter, box-plot, violin, CDF) and automatic export to Excel for the numbers behind every image.

New

Real-time & embedded

Analysis at the point of acquisition.

Low-latency inference for live-stream processing, plus live monitoring with action triggers — pause acquisition and notify the moment a condition is met — deployable even where the computational footprint is constrained.

Plus the building blocks every pipeline leans on

Channel and colour operations, lookup tables, geometry and alignment, type conversion, and pixel-wise image maths and compositing — the connective tissue that lets any workflow fit together exactly as your problem requires.

Modular by design — start with what today’s work needs and add modules as the problem grows, every one running locally on your own machines. See the full OptiFlow platform.

Reproducible & auditable

Results you can defend.
Methods you can hand over.

Modern image analysis is too complex to write into a methods section — and in bespoke work, the method is the deliverable. OptiFlow records it for you, so every result is reproducible, every method is portable and submittable as-is, and your data never leaves your control.

Embedded, tamper-evident provenance

Every result carries its full recipe.

The complete pipeline — every operation, parameter, calibration and operator — is written into the image itself, encrypted and readable only inside OptiFlow. An encrypted, rollback-resistant timestamp guards the record; where it matters, lock it to a password only your team holds.

An exportable method, on demand

Submit it, share it, archive it.

Export the complete pipeline as a standalone file — to attach to a submission, hand to a partner, or archive against a result. Anyone you give it to can reproduce the exact analysis, down to the parameter.

Air-gapped & 100% local

No image ever leaves the machine.

Processing reads and writes your own filesystem directly. No image data crosses the network — no cloud account, no exceptions — so sensitive imagery and unpublished results stay exactly where they belong.

See it: the exact method, recovered from any result.

Every result already contains the workflow that made it — so revisiting your own analysis, handing it to a partner, satisfying an auditor, or re-running across an archive needs nothing rebuilt and nothing re-documented.

Built on science.
Proven by building the new.

The techniques underneath — unsupervised anomaly detection, learned reconstruction, image-based classification — are domain-independent, grounded in peer-reviewed research and already running in production.

Reference-free anomaly detection
Defect screening with no golden reference
An unsupervised pipeline that learns normal appearance from a lab’s own data and flags deviations — no defect library required — triaging hundreds of objects down to a handful for expert review. The same approach transfers to inspection in any field.

Label-free single-cell classification
