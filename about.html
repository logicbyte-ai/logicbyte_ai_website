Why we exist

Your expertise is the scarce resource.
Spend it where you add the most value — not the tooling.

A domain expert’s time is their most valuable asset — yet too much of it is lost to endless iteration and optimisation of image-analysis tooling, rather than the work only they can do. Brittle scripts, one-off macros and manual steps that break the moment the sample, the instrument or the person changes.

Logic Byte turns that work into something durable. We productise image-processing and analysis workflows so they run robustly at scale, reproduce exactly, and hand over to a colleague intact — the expertise captured in the pipeline, not trapped in one person’s head. Every step is recorded in an audit trail you can export and defend. Underneath it all, the latest advances in AI and computer vision — validated on real data long before they touch yours.

The journey

From a lab bench to production.
Built deliberately.

2019

The problem, seen every day

In a university microscopy lab, one pattern kept repeating: the specialists gathering the data were world-class in their fields — cell biology, pathology, materials science, semiconductor fabrication — yet spent their hours wrestling with image processing rather than their actual work. The bottleneck was never the instrument or the data. It was the pipeline between raw image and real insight.

2021

Logic Byte is founded

Founded to close that gap, drawing on years of research in autonomous microscopy, deep learning and computer vision. The premise was simple: put sophisticated, domain-aware image analytics in the hands of the domain expert — no code required — so the platform handles the pipeline and the expert focuses on the work that matters.

2023

Incorporated in Singapore

Logic Byte Pte. Ltd. is incorporated, and the platform is built out methodically — each capability validated against real imaging problems, from enhancement and segmentation to single-cell analysis, defect detection and label-free virtual staining (CytoByte).

Today

In production, internationally

OptiFlow runs in production at ETH Zürich, is in trial at NTU, and now spans three domains — biomedical & life sciences, semiconductor & industrial imaging, and bespoke work across any field. What began as a research problem is now a platform in daily use.

The founder

From fundamental research to productised enterprise deployment.

Dr. Doorgesh Sharma Jokhun
PhD, National University of Singapore · AI & Computer Vision

Doorgesh completed his PhD and postdoctoral research at NUS in computational and biomedical imaging — spanning autonomous microscope control, biosensor CNN development, in-silico (virtual) nuclear staining and large-scale single-cell analysis — resulting in more than twenty peer-reviewed publications, cited more than 1,200 times, across signal processing, deep learning and biomedical imaging.

He then moved into industry, working directly in high-precision semiconductor image analytics — where measurement accuracy, throughput and production reliability are non-negotiable. That trajectory — from fundamental research and biomedical imaging through to semiconductor production — is rare, and it is what gives OptiFlow its unusual breadth without sacrificing depth.

Seeing world-class teams held back by image-processing bottlenecks — not by their instruments, and not by their expertise — was the direct motivation for Logic Byte: to turn research-validated imaging AI into robust tools the experts can actually use.
