The Platform

Built for quality.
Engineered for scale.

OptiFlow is a modular, modality-agnostic platform for building and deploying domain-aware image-analytics workflows — at any scale.

Visual workflow builder
Drag, connect, and configure processing nodes. Save workflows as templates and re-deploy across datasets in one click.
Image enhancement & correction
Illumination correction, noise reduction, contrast enhancement, and channel normalisation — applied automatically or on demand.
Object detection & segmentation
Precision segmentation of cells, defects, and structures. Supports supervised learning and unsupervised anomaly detection.
Morphological analysis & statistics
Automated measurements, population statistics, and interactive charts — from object size distributions to spatial clustering maps.
PDF & Excel reporting
One-click report generation with annotated images, tables, and charts. Fully customisable templates for regulatory and internal use.
Modular, domain-aware architecture
Start with the core and plug in domain-aware modules built for your field's specific imaging challenges — CytoByte for label-free biological staining, defect classifiers for wafer inspection, and more.
Reproducible & auditable by design
Every image OptiFlow produces carries the full sequence of operations and parameters applied to the source — built for record-keeping, audit, and reproducibility.

Every deployment is different. OptiFlow's module library lets you compose exactly the pipeline your workflow demands.

01
Label-free microscopy (CytoByte)
In-silico staining from DIC images to pseudo-DAPI. Visualise nuclear morphology without chemical stains.
02
Single-cell analysis & counting
Automated detection, segmentation, and enumeration of individual cells across large image datasets.
03
Classification & grading
Supervised deep learning models for cell type classification, disease grading, and phenotypic scoring.
04
Supervised defect detection
Train on your own defect library. Achieve human-expert accuracy on wafer, PCB, and material surface inspection.
05
Unsupervised anomaly detection
No labelled defects needed. Detect novel anomalies using learned representations of normal surface appearance.
06
Augmented reality overlay
Real-time AR annotation of live microscopy feeds — highlight regions of interest directly on the instrument view.
FEATURED MODULE
CytoByte — Label-free staining
CytoByte renders pseudo-DAPI nuclear staining directly from standard DIC microscopy images using a trained deep neural network — no chemical preparation, no photobleaching, no additional equipment. Ideal for live-cell imaging, high-content screening, and time-sensitive assays.
