The problem

Imaging data is exploding.
The bottleneck is everything after the capture.

01

Experts stuck in the weeds

Your most expensive people lose hours to manual groundwork that won't scale — dragging whole projects as the backlog piles up faster than they can clear it.

02

Results that can't be defended

When a result can't be traced or reproduced without someone's personal notes, it can't be trusted — or defended in a review, audit, or paper.

03

Everything waits on one person

When the one specialist who can build the pipeline can't hand it over, they become the bottleneck — everyone else queues, and work that could run in parallel runs in series.

04

Generic AI breaks on domain images

Off-the-shelf models fail on sector-specific imaging — the noise alone is nothing like natural photos — so you're handed wrong answers with false confidence.

The architecture

Built for quality.
Engineered for scale.

OptiFlow is a modular, imaging modality-agnostic platform for building and deploying domain-aware workflows — from a single image to millions. What follows is a snapshot of what's inside.

Explore the full platform

No-code workflow builder

Compose, validate, and deploy complex pipelines visually. Branch, preview any step, and run on batches or live feeds — no code at any stage.

Domain-aware AI

Models trained on domain-specific data — SEM, TEM, optical microscopy, industrial imaging — not generic models repurposed to fit.

Built for handoff

A pipeline built by a specialist becomes a portable artefact. Hand it to a domain expert who runs it at scale and reads results in their own language.

Integrity by design

100% local, air-gapped deployment — no image ever leaves the machine. Every output carries a tamper-evident record of the sequence of operations, parameters, and operator that produced it.

The library. Use only what you need.

Restoration, detection, segmentation, classification, quantification, and reporting — domain-aware modules you can preview, then licence only what your workflow needs.
